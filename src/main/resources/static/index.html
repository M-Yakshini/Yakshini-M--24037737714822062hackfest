TROUBLESHOOTER

Common Errors & Fixes

🔴
"Session expired" error

Your login session timed out.

✅ Fix: Sign out and sign back in. Your saved sketches are preserved.

🟡
Variations not loading / blank images

The sketch dataset may not be accessible.

✅ Fix: Refresh the page. If the problem persists, contact your administrator to verify the dataset path is correctly configured.

🟡
"Sketch exceeds 5 MB" save error

The selected variation is too large to save.

✅ Fix: Try selecting a different variation. All dataset images are well under 5 MB; this may indicate a network issue.

🔵
Dashboard shows no history

No sketches have been saved yet, or you're not logged in.

✅ Fix: Save at least one variation from the Editor, then click Refresh in the Dashboard.

🔵
Page not loading at all

The local server may have stopped.

✅ Fix: Restart the server and navigate to http://localhost:8080. Contact your administrator if the problem continues.
